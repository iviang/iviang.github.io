  • California close-price model

    IDX Exchange

    Predicts the final sale price of a single-family home in California from CRMLS sold records. Six notebooks take the data from exploration through preprocessing, baselines, gradient-boosted models, and a full metric suite. The deployed app switches between two finalists: a gradient-boosted model on a log target for the luxury tail, and a ridge-stacked ensemble for mid-market homes.

  • Language widgets

    Side project iOS

    iPhone widgets that teach a word a day in Vietnamese, Chinese and Spanish, each with a pronunciation guide, meaning and example sentence. They work offline, change at midnight, shuffle words at random, and open Google Translate when tapped.

    The Chinese widget offers both Cantonese and Mandarin readings, the Vietnamese one primarily uses Southern pronunciation, and the Spanish one uses Latin American Spanish with a word bank focused on healthcare.

    The transparent background for the lock-screen version is adapted from Max Zeryck's Widget Blur technique.

Case studies and writing

  • Honeywell supply chain case study

    162 pages

    A supply-chain design for a hypothesized Honeywell “Distributed Wireless” home security system, from product architecture and subsystems through demand forecasting to the supplier, manufacturing and distribution network. My part was the supply-chain network model, the demand forecast (moving average, Holt and Winters methods) and the NPV base case. Coursework built around a hypothetical product, not work done for Honeywell.

    • Read the full project (PDF)

  • Behind the Streams: Exploring Twitch's Technical Design

    22 pages Research poster

    A technical guide I researched and wrote for three audiences: beginners, streamers and tech enthusiasts. It follows a stream from RTMP ingest through Twitch's Intelligest routing to adaptive HLS playback, then covers the real-time chat system, the inputs behind recommendation models, and compression and latency, with a transcoder case study and original figures. I took it from proposal to document specification to final paper, and presented it as a research poster.
